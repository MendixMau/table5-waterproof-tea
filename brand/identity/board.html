Patience, ritual, harmony.
Opens at the right heat · closes when it is done

Lu Yu read water by its bubbles. The tea bag traded that art for convenience a century ago. OSME brings it back: a bag that stays sealed until the water reaches its tea's ideal temperature, then closes again as the cup cools.
