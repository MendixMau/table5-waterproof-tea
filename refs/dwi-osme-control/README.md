# Dwi's Osme Control concept (PR #1), kept as a reference

- `osme-control-20s.mp4`: a 20-second, 1080p, silent explainer built from crops of the storyboard.
- `osme-control-storyboard.png`: the concept storyboard it is built from.

It shows a manual capsule: you lift a rod to open it and push it down to close it. OSME opens by itself at its tea's temperature, so this film is not used in the pitch. The team took three ideas from it and redrew them in our style:

1. The plume close-up (tea swirling out into clear water), for the film's 80 °C beat.
2. The three-state strip. The site already has one: Sealed · Open · Sealed.
3. The exploded "what's inside" view, which the product thread is building in `shared/product/`.

The lead reviewed it at 11:02; the review is `gallery/pr1-review.png`.
