# Judge: OSME brand film v02

**Verdict:** A big step up from v01. Jurors can now say "a tea bag that opens at the right temperature". It is calm and premium, but it reads as the demo app with a prologue, not a brand film yet.

- **(a) Hook: 3.** Lu Yu's line opens well, but it sits as a static three-dot infographic for 5 s. The bag arrives at 6.3 s and opens at 9.5 s. Patient, but nothing builds.
- **(b) Clarity: 4.** 100° sealed, 80° open, 70° sealed, then the one-liner. Two gaps: "open" is just a thin orange seam on a flat icon, and the labels (about 16 px) are too small for a projector.
- **(c) Story: 3.** Art → bag works: the bubbles fall into the glass and become the bag. The trade-off (the rush, the 100° bag) is missing, and the movement exists only in the tagline.
- **(d) Craft: 3.** The palette, amber→jade arc, glow and Jost lockup are tasteful. Against that: the glass is tiny at 5.5–7 s, the icons are UI-flat, there is a dead one-dot frame at 14.6–15.5 s, and the end card runs 4.5 s.
- **(e) Brand fit: 4.** The quiet host, technology serving the ritual, "scent kept". No life or love yet: no hands, no cup offered.

Risk: the film shows the demo's dial and gives away its reveal.

## Three changes
1. **0–3 s:** the line is on screen from frame 0 and the bubbles sit inside the glass, one after another in about 0.6 s. Cut the beat from 5 s to 3 s.
2. **3–5 s:** use the saved 2 s for the trade-off: an ordinary bag at 100° turns dark, caption "Then the tea bag: fast, always 100°." OSME bag drops in.
3. **9.5 s and 12.5 s:** the bag visibly parts and a jade-gold plume runs into the water, with a 1.15× push-in. On sealing, a jade pulse. Labels go from 16 px to 28 px or more. Morph bag to logo at 14.6 s with no dead frame, and trim the end card to 3 s.
