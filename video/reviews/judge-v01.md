# Judge: OSME brand film v01 (19 s)

**Verdict:** tasteful and on-palette, but it is a logo animation, not a product film. No tea bag, cup, water or leaf ever appears. The "bag" (9.5–15 s) is the logo ring, and it never opens or closes. A juror leaves with "a nice tea brand about temperature", not "a bag that opens at 80 °C".

| | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| (a) Hook, 0–10 s | 2 | ~1 s near-black, then a small top caption and flat dots, one every ~2.5 s. Calm, but nothing to lean in to. |
| (b) Clarity | 2 | "Tea bag" is never on screen. "Sencha opens at 80 °C": what opens? "Closes" is only told in text. |
| (c) Story | 3 | The order is right, and the bubbles flying into the ring (7.5–9.5 s) is the best moment. With no product beat, the film jumps from art to logo. |
| (d) Craft | 3 | Clean type and glow. Stage labels are tiny and grey, the dots have no depth or water, and the arc reads as UI. It feels like a template. |
| (e) Brand fit | 4 | Midnight Brew, quiet, premium, but near "slow and empty". |

## 3 changes with the biggest lift
1. **10–15 s: show the product.** Replace the logo-as-bag with a line-drawn pyramid bag in a glass cup with a water line. At 80 °C the seams part and amber bleeds into the water. The marker goes on to jade (~65 °C), the bag closes and the colour stops. Put the one-liner on screen: "A tea bag that opens only at its perfect temperature." Morph the bag into the logo at 15 s.
2. **0–7.5 s: compress and hook.** No dark frames: a bubble is on screen at 0.0. Centre the caption at about double the size (~56 px). Bring the bubbles in 1 s apart so the art beat ends by 5 s.
3. **Make it water, not slides.** Bubbles rise with a wobble, breathe in size, carry slight blur and a faint caustic texture. Labels go from ~14 px grey to ~28 px warm white at 80%.
