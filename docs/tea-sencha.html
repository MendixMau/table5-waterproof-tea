The tea

Sencha is the cup Japan drinks every day: bright, grassy, a little sweet. Boiling water makes it bitter. The bag stays sealed until the water cools to 80 °C, brews, and seals again below 70 °C. This is the tea in our live demo.

Its culture · Japan

Chanoyu and the everyday cup of Sencha. Ichi-go ichi-e: “each meeting should be treasured, for it can never be reproduced.”

Stops itself

Opens in its window, closes as the water cools. Never stewed, never bitter.

Scent kept

Sealed between cups, so the leaf keeps its scent for the next infusion.

Up to 3 cups

One bag brews up to 3 times: about $0.50 a cup.

Questions

Good to know.

How does the bag know the temperature?

The principle exists: lab membranes already open and close with temperature. Our R&D makes it food-safe at 70–90 °C. Each tea’s bag is tuned to its own window.

Do I need a special kettle or mug?

No. Pour from any kettle into any mug. The bag waits for its window, brews, and seals again as the water cools. No thermometer, no timer.

How many cups from one bag?

Up to 3 infusions, about $0.50 a cup. Between cups the bag stays sealed, so the leaf keeps its scent.
