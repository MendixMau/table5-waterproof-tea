Our story

1,200 years of temperature.

Every great tea culture learned to read its water. The tea bag made tea easy and forgot the art. OSME brings the art back, inside the bag.

760s

Lu Yu reads the water

China’s Classic of Tea judges water by its bubbles: three stages, three moments.

“The first boil is when the little bubbles like the eye of fishes swim on the surface.”
1908

The tea bag trades art for ease

Thomas Sullivan’s silk bags make tea fast. One temperature, no stop, one cup. Today 96% of UK tea is brewed from a bag.

Now

The art, back in the bag

A bag that knows its tea: it opens at the right heat, closes when done, and keeps the scent. Any kettle, any mug.

The name

OSME. Greek for scent.

The right temperature opens a tea’s scent; the wrong one changes it. We named the brand after what we protect: the scent of every tea, kept.

HonestThe principle exists: lab membranes already open and close with temperature. Our R&D makes it food-safe at 70–90 °C. Up to 3 infusions per bag.

Every tea has its temperature. A tea bag that opens only at its perfect temperature.
