# OSME pitch: jury Q&A (10 likely questions)

Status: v1 (presentation-script-v2-builder, 27 Nov 2026). One-line answers of 25 words or fewer; "Behind it" says where the proof sits. Any speaker may answer; D (the ask) takes money questions, B takes the science.

## 1. Is the membrane food-safe? What is it made of?
Answer: Not yet: the raise funds it. A food-grade cellulose coating, tuned to 70 to 90 °C, then certified in EU, US and Japan.
Behind it: slide 8 (use of funds: R&D 35%, certification 15%); facts.md "R&D framing"; truth/business-case.md §3 (hydroxypropyl cellulose).

## 2. Does it really work? How far along are you?
Answer: The principle exists: lab membranes already open and close with temperature. Our R&D makes it food-safe at 70-90 °C. The evidence: PNIPAm-grafted membranes opened and closed with heat over five clean cycles in the lab (PMC9920928); they switch at 32 °C, so tuning them per tea and making them food-safe is the R&D the raise funds.
Behind it: slide 3 notes; truth/history-cultures.md (PMC9920928, plain PNIPAm switches at 32 °C); facts.md "R&D framing".

## 3. What does a bag cost you at scale?
Answer: $0.42 a bag at year-3 scale, against $1.27 in the year-1 pilot. The membrane material itself is under one cent a bag.
Behind it: slide 6; truth/business-case.md §3 (cost stack); deck/model_business.py cogs().

## 4. Why $1.50 and not $2.00?
Answer: $1.50 makes the first cup an easy yes at $0.50 a cup. At $2.00 it is still $0.67, below every premium rival; the team decides.
Behind it: slide 6; truth/business-case.md §6 (price sensitivity: LTV:CAC 1.9x at $1.50, 3.3x at $2.00).

## 5. Why a subscription?
Answer: Ritual turns one cup into a habit: 16 bags a month, $24, skip or pause anytime. It repays acquisition in 7.7 months.
Behind it: facts.md "Price"; truth/business-case.md §4 (contribution $9.78 a month, CAC payback).

## 6. What stops a big brand copying you?
Answer: The tuned membrane is the product, so 35% of the raise goes to membrane R&D and IP. Brand, origin partners and Ritual members follow.
Behind it: slide 8 (use of funds); slide 7; truth/customers-market.md §4 (why OSME wins).

## 7. Is the market real? Why $640 m?
Answer: $7.1 bn is global tea bag spend; $640 m is its premium share in Japan, China, UK and US. Year 3 needs only 1.3% of it: about 205 k buyers, 0.13% of the 159 m Americans who drink tea every day.
Behind it: slide 5; truth/business-case.md §1 (Fortune Business Insights 2025, shares labelled ASSUMPTION) and §5 (bottom-up $8.3 m).

## 8. What do you do with $2.5 m, and what are the milestones?
Answer: 18 months to a food-safe membrane at 70 to 90 °C, then the first Ritual boxes. Series A at 20 k subscribers.
Behind it: slide 8; truth/business-case.md §7 (use of funds 35 / 15 / 30 / 15 / 5, 24 months runway; Series A trigger 20 k subscribers or $5 m run-rate).

## 9. Why a $10 m pre-money valuation?
Answer: Carta's Q3 2025 median seed pre-money is $16 m; as a pre-revenue brand with R&D ahead, we price below it: $12.5 m post, 20% dilution.
Behind it: slide 8 notes; truth/business-case.md §7 (Crowdfund Insider on Carta Q3 2025).

Answer: Four of us: brand, product, market and finance. We will hire membrane chemistry with the raise; R&D and IP is the largest budget line.
