## Script

**0:00 to 0:25 · Slide 1 · Company · Speaker A**
Cue to next slide: "opens only at its perfect temperature." (A clicks)
[Brand title card "The Art of Tea. 1,200 years of temperature." for 4 s, then the 18 s film plays silent behind A: Lu Yu's three boils, the 1908 bag, the lockup. The year marker runs 760, 1908, Today. Speak calmly; the film ends on the one-liner as A says it.]
The art of tea: twelve hundred years of temperature.
In the year seven sixty, in China, Lu Yu read the heat of water at a glance.
Fish eyes. A string of pearls. Rolling waves.
His Classic of Tea made tea an art.
We are OSME. Oz-may. Greek for scent.
A tea bag that opens only at its perfect temperature.

**0:25 to 0:45 · Slide 2 · Problem · Speaker A**
Cue to next slide: "a forgotten bag: bitter." (A clicks)
[The scent thread travels China, Japan, Britain to the 1908 bag; the 1908 cup turns bitter at 5:40, then 96% lands; OSME is not shown until the demo. About 9 s, no clicks.]
Twelve centuries of ritual.
Then, around nineteen oh eight, we traded the art for convenience: the tea bag.
Ninety-six percent of UK tea is brewed from a bag.
Boiling water on green tea, a forgotten bag: bitter.

**0:45 to 1:25 · Slide 3 · Solution, live demo · Speaker B**
Cue to next slide: "food-safe at seventy to ninety degrees." (B presses PageDown)
[3 s intro: the hero cup, alive, and "Sealed. Open. Sealed." Then B drives the laptop. Sencha is preselected. B presses Space to pour boiling water. OSME bag in the big cup, the 1908 bag on the right. Leave the pauses: the jury watches the dial and the cups.]
Sealed. Open. Sealed. Let me show you. Sencha in boiling water, cooling sped up.
On the right, an ordinary bag brews at once.
In the middle, OSME. Sealed. It waits.
[Pause until the dial reaches 80.]
Eighty degrees: it opens.
[Pause until the dial drops below 70.]
Below seventy, it seals again. The steep stops itself.
The ordinary bag turns bitter. Ours keeps its scent for a second cup, and a third.
[If time allows, press Space again to show the second brew; R resets.]
The principle exists: lab membranes already open and close with temperature. Our R&D makes it food-safe at seventy to ninety degrees.

**1:25 to 1:50 · Slide 4 · Customer · Speaker C**
Cue to next slide: "already brew loose leaf." (C clicks)
[The amber wisp draws from each quote into the bag.]
Everyone can be a tea person. We start with the curious specialty drinker, twenty-five to forty-five, who already pays for good leaves.
One wrote: "I spend way too much on tea to just throw them away after one steep."
Another: "My leaves start not tasting as fresh when I reuse them from the day before."
Sixteen percent of UK black tea drinkers already brew loose leaf.

**1:50 to 2:05 · Slide 5 · Market · Speaker C**
Cue to next slide: "two hundred thousand buyers." (C clicks)
Tea bags: a seven point one billion dollar market.
We start with premium in four markets: six hundred and forty million.
Year three: eight point three million, from about two hundred thousand buyers.

**2:05 to 2:20 · Slide 6 · Business · Speaker C**
Cue to next slide: "seventy-two percent gross margin." (C clicks)
One bag is a dollar fifty, in every pack, and brews up to three cups: fifty cents a cup.
At year-three scale we keep a dollar eight a bag: seventy-two percent gross margin.

**2:20 to 2:35 · Slide 7 · Competition · Speaker D**
Cue to next slide: "the raise pays for that." (D clicks)
Every way of tea gets something right.
Only OSME brings art and ease into one bag, no machine.
Where we lose today: the first cup costs more, and the membrane still needs certification. The raise pays for that.

**2:35 to 3:00 · Slide 8 · The ask · Speaker D**
Cue to next slide: "Series A." (D clicks once more: logo, "Every tea has its temperature." and the waitlist QR)
[Use of funds stays on screen, not spoken: 35 / 15 / 30 / 15 / 5.]
We ask two and a half million dollars on a SAFE, at a ten million dollar pre-money valuation.
Eighteen months to a food-safe membrane at seventy to ninety degrees. Then the first Ritual boxes ship, and at twenty thousand subscribers, Series A.
[D clicks: logo and tagline.]
Every tea has its temperature. We are OSME. Scan the code and join the waitlist.
