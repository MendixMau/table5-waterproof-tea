# The brief

**Sell the unsellable.** Table 5: Waterproof Tea Bags. Build 10:25 to 13:30, pitches 13:35, done 14:30.
Any language. Everyone drives at least once.

1. **Brand**: a name, a logo and one line that says what it is.
2. **Customer**: who would buy this? Show where you found them.
3. **Product**: something that works today, on one laptop.
4. **Site**: one site that holds it all: what it does, who it is for, the price.
5. **Pitch**: three minutes, with a full pitch deck and the product running. Eight slides:
   company · problem · solution · customer evidence · market · business · competition · the ask.
