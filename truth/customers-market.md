## 1. Consumer complaints (real quotes)
- Forgotten bag: "FUCK I'VE BEEN STEEPING THIS FOR 20 MINUTES THIS IS GOING TO MELT MY TONGUE." (weland, Hacker News) https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=7725580
- Forgotten bag: "it does occasionally happen that I drop the bag or the leaves in, start debugging." (weland, same thread)
- Scorched green tea: "Pour boiling water over green tea and you'll scorch the leaves, releasing harsh tannins that overpower the natural sweetness." (The Tea Spot, side-by-side test: boiling version "noticeably more bitter") https://www.theteaspot.com/blogs/steep-it-loose/green-tea-done-right
- Wasting good leaves: "I spend way too much on tea to just throw them away after one steep!" (hopeofdawn, TeaChat) https://www.teachat.com/viewtopic.php?p=212298
- Aroma lost: "my leaves start not tasting as fresh when I reuse them from the day before." (silversun, TeaChat, same thread)
- Bag second cup: "some tea tastes bitter the second time around with the bag." (leroy_the_mule, Straight Dope) https://boards.straightdope.com/t/do-you-re-use-tea-bags/85495
- Waste: "I suppose I'm wasteful because I use 1 bag per cup." (Violet, same thread)
- Bag waste/plastic: one plastic tea bag at brewing temperature released ~11.6 billion microplastic and 3.1 billion nanoplastic particles (McGill, Env. Sci. & Tech. 2019) https://reporter.mcgill.ca/plastic-teabags-release-billions-of-microscopic-particles-into-tea/
- Note: Reddit itself was not reachable for search; quotes come from HN, TeaChat, Straight Dope and a tea retailer test.

Slide-ready line: people love tea, but the bag forgets the temperature, forgets the time, and throws the leaf away after one cup.

## 2. Two strongest consumer segments
**A. The curious specialty drinker (global, 25-45, loose leaf, matcha, origin teas)**
- Specialty tea USD 38.89 bn (2025) -> 76.51 bn (2034), CAGR 7.81%; buyers moving "toward premium, origin-specific, and artisanal" teas; wellness and younger buyers named as drivers (Fortune BI) https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/specialty-tea-market-111115
- UK: 16% already brew black tea loose leaf (UKTIA 2024) https://www.teaandcoffee.net/?p=34292
- Younger, green and matcha: 32% of Gen Z daily tea drinkers choose green tea (vs 20% nationally); matcha 7% Gen Z, 9% Millennials vs 4% overall (YouGov UK) https://yougov.com/en-gb/articles/52458-is-britain-still-a-tea-drinking-nation
- Pain fit: they pay for good leaves and hate wasting or scorching them (TeaChat quotes above).
- Rough size: the specialty pool (~USD 39 bn); OSME starts with the green, oolong and matcha-curious slice.

**B. Gift and Ritual-subscription buyers**
- Tea subscriptions USD 530 m (2025) -> 1.2 bn (2035), CAGR 9.1%, driven by "preference for premium tea experiences" (Fact.MR) https://www.factmr.com/report/tea-subscription-market
- "Seven in 10 tea drinkers agree that they would be happy to receive a premium box of tea as a gift." (Mintel UK, ~2016) https://www.mintel.com/insights/food-and-drink/flavoured-teas-could-brew-success-for-brands-this-festive-season/
- "Tea gift sets are among the third most popular food gifting items" (Packaged Facts via World Tea News) https://www.worldteanews.com/Features/tea-gifting-rise
- Pain fit: a gift that shows itself (the bag opening at the right moment) is the demo. Gift box -> first Ritual subscription.
- Rough size: subscriptions ~USD 0.5 bn and growing fastest; gifting is a channel into segment A.

## 4. Competition and why OSME wins
| Today | What it gets right | Where it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Supermarket bags | cheap, fast | one temperature, keeps steeping, one cup then bin, often plastic |
| Pyramid bags (Tea Forte) | whole leaf, gift look | still over-steeps, still one cup |
| Loose leaf + infuser | best taste, multiple infusions | needs timer, thermometer, skill; aroma fades between cups |
| Gaiwan / gongfu | the full art | ritual needs knowledge and time; intimidating to newcomers |
| Variable-temp kettle | right temperature | no timing; ~USD 165-180 (Fellow Stagg, retailer listings, unverified) |
| Capsule machines | push-button | machine, pods, waste; Special.T and Teforia show the limits |
- Teforia: smart infuser launched at $649, listed near $1,000, shut down Oct 2017; could not raise funds to "educate the market" (TechCrunch) https://techcrunch.com/2017/10/27/1000-tea-infuser-startup-teforia-shuts-down
- Nestle Special.T: machine EUR 129, pods EUR 0.35 at launch (New Atlas) https://newatlas.com/nestle-specialt-tea-machine/16883

**Why OSME wins:** the art of the gaiwan with the ease of a bag. The intelligence sits in the bag, not in a machine or an app: any kettle, any mug, no learning curve. It opens at the right temperature, stops itself, seals the scent and brews again. Lesson from Teforia: hardware that needs explaining dies; OSME needs no explaining, you watch it open.
