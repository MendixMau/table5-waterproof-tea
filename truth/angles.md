# Waterproof tea bags: top 5 angles

Scores run 1 to 5: S = survives normal use, C = customer with money, D = laptop demo in 3 minutes, M = memorable.

Recommendation: lead with 1, use 2 as the business model slide, use 4 as the live demo moment.

## 1. SteepLock: the tea bag with a stop button
**Pitch:** "Every cup steeped exactly as long as you choose."
**Mechanism:** Pull the string and a drawstring pulls the leaves into a sealed waterproof chamber, so steeping stops. The user controls it; temperature plays no part.
**Customer:** Hotel buffets, office pantries and café chains, who all pay for consistent quality.
**Demo:** A browser simulator. The cup darkens live, you press STOP, and the bitterness meter freezes at "perfect" while the regular cup next to it turns black.
**Scores:** S5 C4 D5 M4 (18)
**Weakest point:** "Just take the bag out." Answer: nobody does. Show "average forgotten bag: 11 minutes" on the evidence slide.

## 2. The bag is the sachet: zero-waste, humidity-proof tea
**Pitch:** "We deleted the foil wrapper. The bag protects itself."
**Mechanism:** An airtight waterproof skin. Tear the tab and it becomes a normal permeable bag. No foil, and it stays fresh in tropical humidity.
**Customer:** Airlines and hotel chains, who pay for galley space, weight and ESG reporting.
**Demo:** A calculator: "40M sachets a year: 212 tonnes of foil saved."
**Scores:** S5 C5 D4 M3 (17)
**Weakest point:** "A wrapper with extra steps." Answer: a wrapper you never throw away, plus a number for the annual report.

## 3. DryDesk: the only tea bag safe next to your laptop
**Pitch:** "Zero drips from cup to bin."
**Mechanism:** The shell seals when you lift the bag out, so no liquid escapes.
**Customer:** IT and facilities teams. Liquid spills are a leading cause of laptop damage.
**Demo:** Put a "used" bag on the demo laptop's keyboard live, then show the slide "€0 in repairs."
**Scores:** S4 C3 D5 M5 (17)
**Weakest point:** A gimmick with a small budget line. Answer: bundle it into the SteepLock office deal.

## 4. Pure Ritual: tea without the tea
**Pitch:** "All the mindfulness of tea. None of the caffeine, tannins or stains."
**Mechanism:** It never opens. You dunk it, wait and do the ritual, and the water stays pure. One bag lasts a lifetime, sold as a ritual subscription.
**Customer:** Corporate wellbeing budgets, sleep clinics, wellness apps.
**Demo:** A 3-minute "steep meditation" web app. The water stays clear and the screen says "Ritual complete."
**Scores:** S5 C2 D5 M5 (17)
**Weakest point:** "You're selling hot water." Answer, straight-faced: "Correct. At 94% margin."

## 5. NoonTea: drop it in at 8, drink at 12
**Pitch:** "The first tea bag with a schedule."
**Mechanism:** A coating that dissolves in cold water, colour-coded in 2h, 4h and 6h release bands. You get cold brew at noon from your morning bottle.
**Customer:** Commuters, gyms, the premium cold-brew market.
**Demo:** A timeline slider: the bottle is clear at 08:00 and amber at 12:00.
**Scores:** S4 C3 D4 M4 (15)
**Weakest point:** "What about hot water?" Answer: it opens at once and works as a normal bag. The fallback is the feature.

**Also considered:** disaster and military kits (flood-proof, 10-year shelf life), a multi-chamber cartridge (three steeps on demand), hidden-message gift bags, divers and sailors, a "reseal and save it for later" second steep.
