# The Art of Tea: one storyline for the film, the deck and the site

**1,200 years of temperature.** Every great tea culture learned to read its water. The tea bag made tea easy and forgot the art. OSME brings the art back, inside the bag.

The site's "Our story" page tells this best, and the team loves it. The film and the deck tell the same five beats, in this order, with these words. The facts come from `truth/history-cultures.md` and `facts.md`; the sources are listed on `docs/story.html`.
