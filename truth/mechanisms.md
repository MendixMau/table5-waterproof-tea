# Waterproof tea bags: borrowed mechanisms, ranked

The idea behind all six: waterproof means **the tea bag does nothing until you tell it to**. The user sets the trigger by twisting, pulling, pinching or snapping it, or by choosing a time or an ingredient. Water temperature is never the trigger.

## 1. Brew Dial (from Cirkul flavor cartridges). DEMO: app
- **Mechanism:** you turn a dial on the lid, and the dial changes how much flavour the water picks up from a sealed cartridge (Cirkul suggests starting at level 3).
- **Tea feature:** a twist collar on the tag opens the waterproof skin in steps from 0 (sealed) to 5. Set the strength, get the same cup every time, and turn it back to 0 to save the bag for a second cup.
- **Demo:** a slider plus a cup that changes colour.
- Source: https://drinkcirkul.com/pages/getting-started

## 2. Pull to Stop (from the Bodum Assam tea press). DEMO: app
- **Mechanism:** you press the plunger and the brewing stops ("press the plunger down to stop the brewing process").
- **Tea feature:** pull the string and a drawstring closes the waterproof shell around the leaves. Waterproofing becomes the stop button: no bitter, over-steeped cup when a meeting runs long, and the bag can stay in the mug.
- **Demo:** a timer app with a SEAL button that freezes the colour.
- Source: https://www.target.com/p/-/A-85071369

## 3. Pinch Booster (from Karma Push Cap / VIZcap vitamin caps). DEMO: app or paper prototype
- **Mechanism:** active ingredients stay sealed in the cap until you press it, because vitamins and probiotics break down in liquid. They are released "just seconds before consumption".
- **Tea feature:** a second, waterproof pod holds vitamin C, ginger, honey or a matcha shot. Pinch it to add it to this cup. The actives stay fresh.
- **Demo:** a "build your cup" app with toggles.
- Source: https://www.packaginginsights.com/news/instant-mix-cap-karma-aptar-collaboration-targets-personalized-fortified-nutrition-trend.html

## 4. Snap to Brew (from instant cold packs and self-heating cans). DEMO: physical prototype
- **Mechanism:** the contents sit in an inner sealed bag or behind a membrane. You squeeze it, pull a ring, or push a rod through the membrane, and the two parts mix.
- **Tea feature:** drop the sealed bag into your cold water bottle in the morning and snap it when you want tea. Cold brew on demand for a commute, hike or flight.
- **Demo:** a ziplock bag inside a ziplock bag in a glass of water.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_pack and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-heating_food_packaging

## 5. Two-Act Tea / time lock (from Pulsincap delayed-release capsules and slow-melt PVA fishing bags). DEMO: app
- **Mechanism:** a swelling plug, or a slower-dissolving film, holds the contents back until a set delay has passed. Korda sells a slower-melting bag so the bait reaches deep water before it is released.
- **Tea feature:** compartment A brews straight away. Compartment B (mint, spice, or a colour cue that means "ready") opens after a few minutes. The bag tells you when it is done.
- **Demo:** a timeline animation.
- Sources: https://kordatackle.com/knowledge/solid-pva-bags-are-a-very-effective-spring-tactic (Pulsincap comes from pharma literature, found by search only)

## 6. Milk-Smart pocket (from enteric coatings, e.g. EUDRAGIT L 100, which releases above pH 6.0). DEMO: app simulation only
- **Mechanism:** a polymer coat stays shut in acidic conditions and dissolves above a set pH.
- **Tea feature:** adding milk (pH about 6.7) raises the pH and opens a chai-spice or sweetener pocket. Lemon could open a different, acid-soluble pocket. The bag reacts to how you take your tea.
- **Caveat:** whether milky tea actually goes above pH 6 is not verified. Best kept as the "future roadmap" slide.
- Source: https://www.evonik.com/en/products/hc/pr_52000884.html

## Notes
- Freshness is a bonus claim that fits every idea. Nespresso uses a sealed aluminium capsule because it shields coffee from "oxygen, moisture, and light", so a waterproof bag also keeps tea fresh: https://www.contact.nespresso.com/faq/us/en/detail/11308
- Ideas 1 and 2 can be combined: dial the strength, pull to stop. The story is "you control the tea, not the clock".
